The Programme · 18 to 23 November 2026

Six days, chapter by chapter

From the first welcome lunch to the farewell brunch, here is how the Bi-Annual Meet unfolds.

Wed18Nov

Chapter 01 · Arrivals

Check in, and a first evening together

Arrive in Dubai, settle in, and gather for a welcome lunch before the pre-event dinner in Emirates Hills.

  • Check-in · Habtoor Palace / V Hotel
  • Welcome lunch · il Pastaio
  • Pre-event dinner · Ramesh Ramakrishnan's residence, Emirates Hills

Self-transport to the pre-event dinner.

Thu19Nov

Chapter 02 · Opening

Keynotes and the industry round tables

The programme opens with keynote speakers and round tables across ten sectors, then a networking evening in the ballroom.

  • Keynote speakers
  • Industry round tables · ten sectors
  • Networking reception & dinner · V Hotel ballroom

Dress code for the evening: Smart Casual.

Fri20Nov

Chapter 03 · Conference I

The classroom by day, the Palm by night

The first HBS case session with Prof. V. G. Narayanan and founder stories, followed by a Bollywood gala on Palm Jumeirah.

  • HBS Session I · Prof. V. G. Narayanan
  • Founder stories
  • Bollywood Gala · Taj Exotica, Palm Jumeirah

Spouse & partner programme: full day across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Sat21Nov

Chapter 04 · Conference II

The case study and a marquee keynote

A second session in the HBS classroom and a marquee keynote, closing with dinner at Signor Sassi.

  • HBS Session II · the case study
  • Marquee keynote
  • Dinner · Signor Sassi

Spouse & partner programme: full day across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Sun22Nov

Chapter 05 · Dubai Day

A day at leisure

Time to explore the city at your own pace, with an evening at La Perle.

  • Day at leisure · explore Dubai
  • La Perle · evening show
Mon23Nov

Chapter 06 · The Finale

The headline closer and a farewell

A send-off to celebrate the memories, the laughter and the company.

  • The headline closer · closing keynote
  • Farewell brunch

Headline closer to be announced. Checkout after the brunch.

Timings, venues and further speakers to be confirmed.
